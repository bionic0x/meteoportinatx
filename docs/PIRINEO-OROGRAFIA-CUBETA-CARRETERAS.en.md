# Pyrenees Nowcast · fine terrain, cold pools and roads (EXPERIMENTAL)

**Language / Idioma:** [Español](PIRINEO-OROGRAFIA-CUBETA-CARRETERAS.md) · English

**Status:** implemented; tested with synthetic data and one manual live run (25-11-2026). Internal review with agents (code, physics and claim verification), which is not a validation: what was applied and what remains are in §2 and §6. **No parameter is calibrated against observations.** None of this changes warnings, levels or the existing 0–72 h diagnosis: these are three new `core` blocks with their own status. It does not replace AEMET, Meteocat, the Andorran SMA, Météo-France or the traffic services.

## 1. What is added

| `core` block | Module | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| `orografia` | `src/pirineo_orografia.py` | Effective snowflake elevation per point (now and 24 h minimum over hours with precipitation): the profile elevation minus melting under local precipitation enhanced by terrain |
| `cubeta_fria` | `src/pirineo_orografia.py` | Estimated minimum in basin floors that 2–9 km models do not resolve (Das, Puigcerdà, Llívia, Bellver, Formiguères...) |
| `carreteras.oficial` | `src/pirineo_carreteras.py` | Official incidents from SCT and Bison Futé; Andorra and Inforoute 66 as links only |
| `carreteras.exposicion` | `src/pirineo_carreteras.py` | Km of each road above the 24 h minimum effective elevation, cold-pool sections and passes |

Terrain tables are static: `src/pirineo_orografia.json` (≈137 kB, a 0.01° ≈ 1 km grid sampled from a 90 m DEM), generated by `scripts/build_orografia_pirineo.py` (numpy and scipy, outside the core). The core uses the standard library only.

## 2. Physics and parameters (HYPOTHESIS)

**Orographic forcing.** Smith and Barstad (2004) linear model via FFT on the Copernicus GLO-90 DEM (0.01° grid, 42.15–43.05 N × 1.15–2.65 E: from the Cadí and Pedraforca to Foix, and from the Pallars and Montcalm to the Canigou), for 16 directions with U = 14 m/s, N = 0.004 s⁻¹ and τ = 1000 s. Moisture by sector (Cw, Hw): N 0.0030/1800; NE 0.0035/2000; E 0.0060/2500; SE 0.0070/3000; S 0.0065/3000; SW 0.0060/3000; W 0.0040/2500; NW 0.0035/2000. Per point it stores forced precipitation (linear-model mm/h, **relative, not forecast**). At run time it is interpolated by the profile's 700 hPa wind direction, scaled linearly with its speed and turned into a **dimensionless enhancement factor**: enhancement = min(2; 1 + 0.5 × forcing / 3 mm/h). The position label comes from the same field: "realce orográfico" (≥ 1 mm/h), "realce débil" or "sin realce (posible sombra)" (< 0.1 mm/h).

**Effective snowflake elevation.** Starts from the profile's `cota_copo_m` (Tw0 − 300 m, which already includes evaporative cooling):

- Melting: −min(400 m, 100·P^0.7), with P = profile hourly precipitation × enhancement, never more than the depth between the profile ground and Tw0. Only if profile precipitation is ≥ 0.1 mm/h: without profile precipitation the terrain melts nothing. With unknown precipitation, melting is UNKNOWN and the status `PARCIAL`; it is not set to 0.
- Foehn is **not** added: each side's profile already carries it and `core.foehn` diagnoses it separately. A first version added it using the local sign of U·∇z; the physics review showed it contradicted the Smith–Barstad field itself (64 of 512 point-direction pairs labelled lee with forcing ≥ 1 mm/h) and its magnitude never exceeded ~70 m.
- The 24 h minimum is taken **only over hours with precipitation** (a dry hour gives no snow level). If there are none, `sin_precipitacion_24h` = true and `min24` = null, with status OK (not UNKNOWN). Only `OK` hours count; if any is `PARCIAL`, the point is `PARCIAL` and its road km count as UNKNOWN.
- On the divide (Envalira, Puymorens) both profiles are used and the lower elevation is taken.

**Cold pool.** Index CPI = (75th percentile of terrain within ~13 km − z)/100 × log10(cold-air drainage area, D8, km²). ΔTmax = 20 °C × CPI/CPI(Das), capped at 20 °C. Estimated minimum = model 24 h minimum − 0.6 × ΔTmax × (1 − mean cloud cover over the 6 previous hours and the hour of the minimum) × calm, with calm = 1 if the 700 hPa wind ≤ 6 m/s and 0 if ≥ 14 m/s. Per model; a model without cloud cover (AROME HD) stays UNKNOWN. Points with CPI < 1 (e.g. Bolquère) are not treated as cold pools (`NO_APLICA`). With the cap, 7 of the 10 basin-floor points get the same ΔTmax as Das: inside the basin the index is nearly binary.

**Known limit of the index.** CPI grows with valley depth. In Andorra's narrow valleys (Escaldes 9.4; Andorra la Vella 6.0) it comes out far above Das (2.9), which contradicts climatology. The cold pool is therefore applied only to basin floors of the Cerdanya, Cerdagne and Capcir (`cubeta_calibrable`); elsewhere the point is published with status `NO_CALIBRADO`. Inside the basin, at 1 km resolution, the index **does not separate Das from its neighbours** (Puigcerdà 2.9; Llívia 4.8; Bellver 4.7). That Das has the lowest mean temperature in Spain is not verified by this work.

## 2 bis. Integrated model: control points, summits and ski resorts

Besides the 32 points in `pirineo.POIS` (which request point forecasts), the table includes **97 additional points** that request no forecast: they use the profile of their side (`norte` = north of the axial crest, with the upper-Ariège profile: Ariège, Donezan and Capcir, even though the Capcir and Donezan drain to the Aude; `sur` = Cerdanya profile; `divisoria` = each hour, the side with precipitation and the lower elevation) and their orographic forcing. Coordinates and elevations from the Open-Meteo geocoder (GeoNames).

- **Ariège and Donezan (north):** Tarascon, Les Cabannes, Luzenac, Ax, Orlu, Ascou, Mérens, L'Hospitalet, Camurac, Mijanès, Quérigut, Col de Pailhères; summits Saint-Barthélemy, Soularac, Mont Fourcat, Tarbésou, Rulhe, Thoumasset, Baxouillade, Nérassol; Montcalm and Pica d'Estats (divide, the NW barrier).
- **Ariège–Andorra–Carol–Capcir divide:** Cabaneta, Fontargente, Escobes, Serrera, Font Blanca, Tristaina, Medécourbe, Puig Pedrós (Lanós), Puig de Coma d'Or, Pic de la Mina, Puig Peric, Pic d'Envalira (GeoNames name and elevation to be verified); Mont-Louis and La Llagonne (Haut-Conflent, Têt–Segre–Capcir saddle).
- **Andorra:** Coma Pedrosa, Casamanya, Estanyó, Pessons; El Tarter, Arinsal, La Cortinada.
- **Cerdanya, Cerdagne, Puigmal and Cadí (south):** Puigpedrós, Tossa Plana de Lles, Calm Colomer, Roc Colom, Noufonts, Infern, Gra de Fajol, Puig Redon, Pic de l'Orri, Comabona, Pedraforca (Berguedà); Martinet, Prullans, Lles, Urús, Bolvir, Guils, Porta and Latour-de-Carol (Carol), Enveitg, Ur, Estavar, Targasonne, Odeillo, Err, Llo, Eyne, Saint-Pierre-dels-Forcats; Toses, Planoles, Ribes and Vall de Núria (Ripollès, with the Cerdanya profile).
- **Capcir (north):** Réal, Puyvalador, Pic de Madres, Puig de la Tossa. **Conflent:** Canigou (easterly context; uses the Cerdanya profile, it has none of its own).
- **17 ski resorts** (`core.orografia.estaciones_esqui`): Ax 3 Domaines, Ascou-Pailhères and Mijanès-Donezan (reference point on the road at base elevation, approximate), Camurac, Les Angles, Formiguères, Puyvalador (closed), Font-Romeu, Pyrénées 2000, Cambre d'Aze, Porté-Puymorens (divide), La Molina, Masella, Grandvalira in two sectors (Pas de la Casa–Grau Roig, divide; Soldeu–El Tarter, south), Ordino Arcalís (divide: its top reaches the crest and gets northern spillover) and Pal Arinsal (south). Each with a reference point, published ski range (base–top, with its source; La Molina and Masella unverified; for Camurac and Formiguères the base is the village elevation, approximate) and the **fraction of the ski vertical above the 24 h minimum effective elevation** ("snow across the resort", "only on the upper part", "rain up to the top"). It is not the piste condition or snow depth.

**Font-Romeu plateau (correction of 26-11-2026):** Font-Romeu, Bolquère, Odeillo, Targasonne, Eyne, Saint-Pierre-dels-Forcats, Cambre d'Aze and the Font-Romeu, Pyrénées 2000 and Cambre d'Aze resorts use the `divisoria` rule, engine points included (the table overrides the zone map). Reason: on the night of 25–26 the model, with the Cerdanya profile, gave "no precipitation" on the plateau; XEMA measured 0 mm at Das and Puigcerdà, but snow settled down to Odeillo from N–NW spillover.

Limits of the integrated model: the vertical profile still comes from two points (upper Ariège and Cerdanya), so the hourly precipitation for the additional points is their profile's, not local; Arcalís is therefore treated as `divisoria`. In the engine, `pirineo.POIS` points keep their zone-map side (`arcalis` south, `porte` north; the geographic review proposes `porte` as divide, pending because it changes the engine's elevations and foehn pair). Road profiles use waypoints: they do not follow hairpins, so pass gradients (Puymorens, Pailhères, Font-Romeu) and km are not the real ones; the Cadí tunnel north portal is approximate. Still missing, as a next step: the Quillane and Chioula passes, Campcardós, the high Cadí summits, Vallter and Plateau de Beille, and the CG-1/N-145 La Seu–Andorra. Summits come out lowered on the ~1 km DEM. On the divide, if one side has no data at an hour (or a whole profile is missing), that hour is `PARCIAL`. Roads take the elevation only from villages, passes and resort bases (`pueblo`, `collado`, `estacion`), never from summits or ski-resort reference points. The global status is OK only if every point is; if any is UNKNOWN or PARCIAL, it is PARCIAL.

## 3. Roads

- **Official.** SCT: `https://www.gencat.cat/transit/opendata/incidenciesGML.xml`. Bison Futé / DIR Sud-Ouest (DATEX II, national network): `.../Evenementiel-DIR/grt/RRN/content.xml`. Box filter 42.15–42.90 N × 1.40–2.30 E. `meteo` is flagged by keywords (snow, ice, chains, WinterDriving...). Both sources are requested in parallel (15 s, no retries). A valid XML without recognisable records is UNKNOWN ("unrecognised format"), not "no incidents"; a record with unreadable coordinates is discarded and counted. 15 min cache with its age in `carreteras.oficial.edad_min`; if both sources fail, the last good response of ≤60 min is served, otherwise UNKNOWN. **UNKNOWN or SIN_FEED does not mean the road is clear.**
- **No open feed:** Mobilitat Andorra (`/totes-incidencies`, yellow, orange, red and black phases) and Inforoute 66 (departmental network: D618, D118...). Link only.
- **Exposure.** 14 roads: CG-2, CG-3, RN22, RN20 Tarascon–Ax–L'Hospitalet, RN20 L'Hospitalet–Puymorens–Porté–Latour–Bourg-Madame, RN116, D618 Ur–Font-Romeu–Perche, D118 Capcir to Puyvalador, D613/D25 Ax–Col de Pailhères–Mijanès (closed in winter), N-260 Toses, N-260 Seu, C-16, Masella access and N-154. Waypoints (±1–2 km; the Ariège, Puymorens, Carol and Pailhères valley sections follow a least-cost path on a 0.0025° grid), sampled every ~0.5 km with the profile fitted to the valley floor and pass elevations forced (Envalira 2408, Pas de la Casa 2085, Puymorens 1915 at 42.560 N 1.8075 E, Toses 1800, Perche 1581, Pailhères 2001). Each sample takes the 24 h minimum effective elevation of the nearest control point (≤15 km); if that point has no precipitation in 24 h it counts in `km_sin_precipitacion`; without data, in `km_unknown`. `km_sobre_cota` only measures falling snow: **it does not cover ice, refreezing, blowing snow or snow already on the ground**. Cold-pool sections: consecutive basin-floor samples with CPI ≥ CPI(Das).

## 4. Regenerating the tables

```bash
python3 scripts/build_orografia_pirineo.py                       # downloads the DEM (Open-Meteo elevation API, with back-off)
python3 scripts/build_orografia_pirineo.py --dem-npz dem.npz --roads-cache roads_cache.json   # with local caches
python scripts/integrity.py --write
```

If `pirineo.POIS` changes, the JSON must be regenerated: points without a table get no diagnosis.

## 5. First live check (25-11-2026, NW flow)

Manual run with no log in the repository (UNKNOWN for third parties). With ARPEGE in the profile and NW flow, the whole Cerdanya floor is "sin realce": Das, Puigcerdà, Bellver, Masella and La Molina, profile elevation 1279 m → 1259 m in the only hour with precipitation (the 20 m are melting with ~0.1 mm/h in the profile). Formiguères (northern profile): 1982 m, 17 h with precipitation. An earlier version gave 1051 m at Masella from a **dry** hour: the bug fixed by the "precipitating hours only" rule. Cold pool with ICON-EU and ~60 % cloud: Das −1.1 → −4.4 °C, Puigcerdà −2.4 → −6.7 °C; Escaldes, Soldeu, Pas de la Casa, Porté and La Molina come out `NO_CALIBRADO`. SCT (209 records, 14 in the box) and Bison Futé (524, 16 in the box) with no weather incidents. These are estimates, not verifications.

## 6. Still missing

- Calibrate melting and cold pool against XEMA (Das, Bellver, Puigcerdà), Météo-France (Mont-Louis, Formiguères) and the SMA. Until then, HYPOTHESIS.
- Melting from an accumulated energy budget (accumulated precipitation × latent heat against the warm layer's heat) instead of the hourly formula: the current one underestimates moderate continuous precipitation in stagnant air and overestimates short showers.
- Cold pool with better predictors: layer stability (T at 700–750 hPa − T2m) as well as wind, snow on the ground, dew point, low cloud or fog, night length and persistence. Check whether Open-Meteo's elevation correction warms valley floors under inversion.
- Cold-pool index on the native 90 m DEM: drainage barrier height (depression filling), flat-floor fraction, sky-view factor, multiscale TPI. The current index (log of drained area) favours downstream points.
- Review in the table: Envalira, Pas de la Casa and Grau Roig get zero forcing between 67.5° and 90°; the linear-model drift (~28 km at 14 m/s) carries precipitation to Das and Alp with southerly wind.
- Console view: the blocks are in `core` but not yet drawn.
- Real road geometry (OSM blocked in this session) and an automatic Andorra feed.
- Southern foehn and cold pools in Andorra and the Carol valley.
