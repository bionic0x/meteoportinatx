**Date: 2026-11-11.** The Pages publication of commit `535c1e4`, cycle `2026-11-11T15:43:48Z`, reports `aemet_cap=sin_clave`, `aemet_obs=sin_clave`, `rayos_mtg_li=sin_clave` (no key). The presence of international sources such as MeteoAlarm does not replace verification of the primary AEMET warning; the UI must identify when it is working only as a fallback.

**AEMET.** Obtain a new API key through the official process, expiring every **three months** under changes announced by AEMET in July–October 2026. Store it only in `Settings > Secrets and variables > Actions > AEMET_API_KEY`, never in public variables, commits, notes or logs. Check the presence summary in `deploy.yml`, then check for `aemet_cap=ok` and the source times and authority, without printing the key. Record expiry outside Git in the Actions variable `AEMET_KEY_EXPIRES` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, not secret), and renew in advance. The daily `credential-expiry.yml` workflow fails, and GitHub notifies you, if the key expires in 14 days or less, has already expired, or is configured without a date; it checks only whether the secret exists and never prints its value. When the API fails, `sin_clave` or `error` is published explicitly, rather than an invented AEMET warning.

**EUMETSAT MTG-LI.** Decision: **do not configure keys or claim live lightning coverage** until the licence and availability of the Lightning Imager Level-2 product, credentials (and their expiry), quotas, product latency, spatial accuracy, redistribution permissions and quality indicators have been confirmed. Additional licences may be required for some Data Store datasets. Lightning must remain separate from rainfall, and `UNKNOWN`/no coverage must not be downgraded to “no lightning”. Specific permission and validation against known lightning events → a new controlled PR; without these, keep it outside the public operational scope.

**Météo-France (DPObs API, real time).** A free key from portail-api.meteofrance.fr is stored as the Actions secret `METEOFRANCE_API_KEY` (connector `src/meteo_france_obs.py`, sent in the `apikey` header). `deploy.yml` passes it to the cycle and reports only its presence. If an expiry date was chosen at creation, record it in `METEOFRANCE_KEY_EXPIRES` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), and `credential-expiry.yml` will warn 14 days beforehand; without a date it only reports. The `meteofrance-probe.yml` workflow checks the key without printing it and reports the HTTP code: 401 = expired or invalid token; 403 = application not subscribed to the “Données d'observation” API or a different credential type. Data are under Licence Ouverte: publication credits Météo-France and shows the time of the latest datum.
